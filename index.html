Dr. Sanket Mishra

Associate Professor | MIT Bengaluru, MAHE · Machine Learning & IoT Security | TCS PhD Research Fellow [2016–2020]

I am an Associate Professor in the School of Computer Engineering at Manipal Institute of Technology Bengaluru (MAHE). My research sits at the intersection of online machine learning, concept drift detection, deep learning, and Internet of Things security — building adaptive, scalable frameworks for real-time intrusion detection on evolving IoT data streams.

Previously an Assistant Professor at VIT-AP University, Amaravati, Andhra Pradesh (2020–2025), I earned my Ph.D. from BITS Pilani, Hyderabad. I have published 37+ peer-reviewed papers with 187+ citations (h-index 8, i10-index 4) in venues including Internet of Things, Cluster Computing, IEEE Access, and Scientific Reports.

Scholar profiles: Google Scholar · ResearchGate · ORCID · Scopus

I work on identifying key challenges in IoT ecosystems and solving them with frameworks that are adaptive, lightweight, and scalable. I am open to research collaborations, Ph.D./M.Tech supervision, and invited talks.

Research areas: IoT intrusion detection · Online ML & concept drift · Streaming analytics & CEP · Smart cities & ITS · Lightweight edge ML · Deep learning & vision · Mobile crowdsensing · Healthcare & environmental IoT

  8. SpectralRepNet: Real-time image dehazing for edge surveillance via structural reparameterization and spectral frequency gating
    Conference
    A. Duddu , A. Mishra , S. Mishra, and 3 more authors
    In IEEE International Conference on Advanced Video and Signal-Based Surveillance (AVSS 2026) , 2026
    Accepted
    Abs

    Proposes SpectralRepNet, a lightweight single-image dehazing network for edge surveillance that combines RepVGG structural reparameterization with spectral gating in the Fourier domain to suppress haze-related frequencies. A multi-term loss balances pixel accuracy, perceptual realism, Fourier-domain fidelity, and structural edge sharpness. On RESIDE-6K, the model reaches 25.03 dB PSNR and 0.9315 SSIM with only 0.38M parameters and 4.48 GFLOPs, achieving 244 FPS on a desktop GPU and 80 FPS on NVIDIA Jetson Nano. Downstream YOLOv8 evaluation on real haze datasets assesses transferability from synthetic to real-world detection.

  9. Demeter: Enhancing paddy disease detection using deep ensembles and few-shot learning
    Conference
    S. Mishra, B. V. Chandrahaas , M. Choudhary , and 2 more authors
    In IEEE International Conference on Advanced Video and Signal-Based Surveillance (AVSS 2026), ISSA Workshop , 2026
    Accepted
    Abs

    Presents Demeter, a deep learning ensemble for multiclass paddy disease detection in IoT-enabled precision agriculture. IoT sensors monitor temperature, humidity, and disease-related variables while intelligent models process imagery to spot early symptoms and support targeted fungicide use. Combines Octave Convolutional Network, DenseNet, MobileNet, and Dual-Path Networks with few-shot learning and a novel model-selection strategy. Evaluated on 16,225 images across 13 disease classes, achieving 97.84% test accuracy and establishing benchmarks for individual models and their ensembles.
